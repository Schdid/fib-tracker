2. Toolkit — what he actually uses

Liquidation heatmaps (Coinglass)

Reads 7-day (LTF) and 30-day (HTF) heatmaps simultaneously. Splits long-side vs short-side liquidation clusters, then trades the asymmetry into the heavier cluster.

"BTC is trapped between long and short liquidation zones in both low and high time frames signaling a moment of market indecision."— @KillaXBT, June 2025 (NewsBTC)
Orderbook delta / spot CVD

Watches spot CVD slope vs price slope. Buyers accumulating during a drop = HTF long signal.

"On this recent drop, orderbook delta has flipped positive. A sign that buyers are accumulating. Since September, every time delta has flipped green we have seen a push upwards on the HTF."— @KillaXBT, Oct 15 2025 (InvestX)
Fair Value Gaps (FVG / imbalance)

ICT-style 3-candle gaps on the daily timeframe. Treats them as price magnets that "eventually get filled to rebalance order flow."

"Daily fair value gap extending down to $113,355 ... such inefficiencies eventually get filled, as price retraces into the zone to rebalance order flow."— Sept 2025
CME gaps

Treats weekend CME gaps as near-guaranteed targets. Quoted historical fill rate: 98% since BTC at $16k.

"Since trading at $16,000, the Bitcoin market has seen 98% of weekend CME gaps filled."— XT.com, Aug 2025
Weekly / monthly opens

Flippable pivots. Above monthly open = bullish bias; failure to hold = trigger to fade. Tracks every weekly open as the immediate level.

"Bitcoin started the month on a strong technical footing, flipping the monthly open at $115,752 into support."— Bitcoinist, Aug 2025
Funding rate

Negative funding + capitulation = contrarian long trigger. Sustained high positive funding = exhaustion warning. Earns funding fees on shorts as part of his thesis.

111-day SMA

Binary trend filter on the daily. Below = no longs / size down. Above = trend reversal confirmed.

Diminishing cycle multiple

Cycle high-to-low decay: 15.5x → 7.6x → 6.3x → 4.5x → 3.25x projected. Divides current ATH ($126k) by 3.25 to project a $38.8k cycle bottom.

7. To become like him — ranked study path

Tailored for someone already running fib + OI + funding + CVD on Binance/Bybit.

  1. Add a Coinglass liquidation heatmap pane to the dashboard. Read 7d + 30d simultaneously. Mark dense clusters as TP/SL targets. Single biggest gap to close.
  2. Add CME futures BTC chart and tag every weekend gap. Maintain a live "unfilled CME gaps" list as TP candidates.
  3. Anchor fib swings to the prior monthly open instead of arbitrary swings. Hypothesis: tighter stops + cleaner confluence.
  4. Spot-CVD vs perp-CVD divergence alerting. Trigger when 4H spot CVD slope is opposite price slope. Killa explicitly trades this.
  5. Mark daily FVGs as a chart overlay (3-candle imbalance gaps). Long when fib 0.5–0.618 zone overlaps an unfilled bullish FVG.
  6. Add 111-day SMA to BTC chart as a binary trend filter. Below = no longs / reduce size.
  7. Adopt the "weekly close" stop rule for HTF swings. Stop using intraday SLs for swings.
  8. Track every monthly open as a flippable pivot. Above = bullish bias; below = bearish.
  9. Reduce leverage on small-range setups. Killa's max 3–4x rule on 4–5% range setups.
  10. Build a personal "scenarios" template. Each Sunday evening: write Scenario 1 / Scenario 2 / Invalidation in his format.
